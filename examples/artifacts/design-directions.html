Three visual directions for one product idea.

Agents can render options side by side so teams can react to the actual surface instead of imagining it from a paragraph.

Editorial field guide

Big type, quiet grid, annotated cards. Best when the product is about reading, critique, and decision making.

  • Fraunces-style headlines
  • Warm paper palette
  • Soft diagrammatic structure

Control room

Dense metrics, dark panels, obvious system state. Best for operations, evals, and incident work.

  • Compact status surfaces
  • High-contrast evidence blocks
  • Fast scanning over ornament

Calm workspace

Minimal framing, generous spacing, clear actions. Best for teams that want the artifact to lead.

  • Low-noise layout
  • Single primary action
  • Review notes near context
